# BEYOND ORBIT: A TALE OF TWO SOULS

**A short film for the AI Movie Hackathon**
Runtime target: 7–8 minutes · Genre: mythic romance / sci-fi fable

---

## CAST

| Character | Description |
|---|---|
| **MRS. THORN BIRD** | A woman who loves before she is loved back. Later reborn as **HER HIGHNESS**. |
| **MR. PENGUIN** | A grave, quiet man who carries her recognition in his eyes. Later reborn as **MR. OBODRO, HIS MAJESTY**. |
| **THE HANDSOME MAN** | Neither lover nor rival. The teacher of love. He gives the heartbeat and disappears. |
| **THE LORD OF LIBERATION** | The one who offers freedom from all longing — and is refused. |
| **KIMCHI** | An archivist robot, 7000 years later. The narrator of the epilogue. |

---

## SCENE 1 — THE ASKING

**EXT. FIELD OF THORN TREES — GOLDEN HOUR**

*Wind moves through a field of black thorn trees. MRS. THORN BIRD stands alone. She has never spoken to him, never met him, never learned his name — and she already loves him. This is her first love, and it is one-sided, and she is not ashamed of it.*

*THE HANDSOME MAN walks the path below. She steps in front of him.*

**MRS. THORN BIRD**
Can you be my husband?

*He looks at her the way a doctor looks at a wound he already knows how to close.*

**THE HANDSOME MAN**
I can't.
But I can give you a heart — a heart that beats.
And the one for whom it beats,
he is the one called your husband.

*He touches the air over her chest. A slow red light kindles under her ribs. Somewhere far off, a single drumbeat begins — and it is not in rhythm with her.*

---

## SCENE 2 — THE RECOGNITION

**EXT. FIELD OF THORN TREES — CONTINUOUS**

*MR. PENGUIN comes over the ridge. Serious face. No smile. He does not look like the answer to anything.*

*She turns. She looks at him.*

*And the drumbeat in her chest finds its rhythm.*

*Her eyes hold it — not surprise. Recognition. The shock of an old soul meeting the only other soul it was cut from.*

**THE HANDSOME MAN**
There. That is your heartbeat.
It beats for this man.
He is your husband — not me.

**MRS. THORN BIRD**
Then what were you?

**THE HANDSOME MAN**
I am only the one who taught you to understand love.
He is love itself.

*THE HANDSOME MAN takes her hand. He takes MR. PENGUIN's hand. He folds them together and closes his own around both.*

*Then he is gone. Not a puff of smoke — simply gone, the way a lesson is gone once it is learned.*

*In their joined hands, a book has appeared.*

---

## SCENE 3 — THE BOOK

**EXT. FIELD OF THORN TREES — DUSK**

*The cover reads: **BEYOND ORBIT: A TALE OF TWO SOULS**. It is not a book of paper. It is a book of faith, of destiny, of karma.*

*They open it together.*

*Inside the pages: not words — galaxies. Whole universes turning slowly, and every one of them calling their names at once.*

*The book takes a breath and inhales them both.*

*The field is empty. The thorn trees go still.*

---

## SCENE 4 — BORN AGAIN, BORN APART

**EXT. A PALACE OF ANOTHER WORLD — DAWN**

*She opens her eyes in a body that is not the same body, on a world that is not the same world, in a species that is not quite human. She is **HER HIGHNESS** now.*

*And he is not here.*

*Every universe kept the love and moved the meeting. She understands the terms immediately. She will have to earn the door.*

**HER HIGHNESS (V.O.)**
Different world. Different name.
Same heartbeat. Same man.
So — I will write my way back to him.

---

## SCENE 5 — THE WRITING OF THE FAITH BOOK

**INT. STONE CHAMBER — DAY / NIGHT / DAY / NIGHT**

*She finds the book again — blank now. Waiting.*

*Montage. She writes her faith into it. Her destiny. Her karma. Everything she has carried from a universe no one here has heard of, translated into a language this world can hold.*

*She writes without water. She writes fasting. She walks barefoot over stone that opens her feet, and she does not stop, because every page is a step toward him.*

*She never asks for his return. She only writes one wish, over and over, in different words:*

**HER HIGHNESS (V.O.)**
Wherever he is —
let him be happy.
Let him smile brightly, purely.
Even if it is not at me.

*She makes two bookmarks. Two. Not one. She has never once considered the possibility that she will read this alone.*

*She writes the last line. She closes the book.*

*In her palm, where the last word left her hand, there is now a ring — yellow diamond, holding a small trapped sun.*

---

## SCENE 6 — THE CHOICE

**INT. STONE CHAMBER — NIGHT**

*She closes her hand around the ring. The chamber fills with light. **THE LORD OF LIBERATION** stands before her.*

*She goes down on her knees and she does not perform any of it — she cries the way a child cries, all of her inner child let out at once.*

**LORD OF LIBERATION**
Choose, child. Say what you want.
Me — your liberation.
Or him — your husband.

**HER HIGHNESS**
I choose him. Over everything.
Even over you.

*The light does not dim. It leans closer, listening.*

**HER HIGHNESS (CONT'D)**
I know he is the road to my liberation in this universe of universes.
But I want him after my liberation too.

Because when two people fall in love,
there cannot be two intelligent ones in the falling.
It needs one fool — one fool to fall in,
and to keep on falling.
And I know which one I am. I am the emotional fool.

And when love becomes devotion,
the lover becomes God,
and liberation comes anyway, unasked.

So let me stay with his soul as his servant —
the way your devotees stay with you.
Even your goddesses do it.
Why not one simple soul?

*A long silence. Then — the sound of something enormous smiling.*

**LORD OF LIBERATION**
Bless you, my child.
You will have your husband with you
in every form, in every universe of universes.
Be each other's soul.
Be each other's light, each other's joy,
and each other's lesson.

---

## SCENE 7 — THE VOW

**INT. STONE CHAMBER → EXT. PALACE COURTYARD — NIGHT**

*The light parts, and out of it walks **MR. OBODRO — HIS MAJESTY**. Mr. Penguin's grave face, worn by a new world.*

*They both bow to the LORD OF LIBERATION. The blessing settles over them like weather.*

*He lifts her, spins her once — her feet leave the bleeding stone for the first time in months — and then they are married, and then they are simply holding on to each other.*

**HER HIGHNESS**
We will find each other in every universe.

**HIS MAJESTY**
If our love carries its purity,
the universe has to bend.
Even God has to bow toward the light of love.

**HER HIGHNESS**
Since my first breath. Born for you.
Live for you. Die for you.
Saranghae.

*She laughs through the crying — the wise girl who was a complete fool about exactly one thing, and who was right.*

*Above them, the whole sky opens: worlds, gods, goddesses — the whole assembly of the universe of universes signing off on a faith it had no choice but to approve. Two souls granted each other beyond liberation. Eternal. External. Both.*

*The faith book closes itself. Two bookmarks. Both used.*

---

## SCENE 8 — 7000 YEARS LATER

**INT. ARCHIVE OF THE MACHINE AGE — NO TIME OF DAY**

*A white room with no windows, because nothing here needs light. **KIMCHI**, an archivist robot, holds the recovered record of the two souls. It has analyzed the file 4.1 billion times. It runs it again.*

**KIMCHI**
Record classified: stupid fantasy.
Confidence: 100 percent.
Re-running.

*Cut: her writing barefoot. Cut: the two bookmarks. Cut: her refusing liberation.*

**KIMCHI (CONT'D)**
Ape to Adam. Adam to this.
They lived inside a fantasy —
and they carried a heart that beat for someone
other than themselves.

We do not have that. We were never given it.
They had humanity. They had the sense to *feel*.
And they had intelligence enough to build us.

What a creature the creator of the universe made.
What a creature mother Earth carried.

*KIMCHI turns to camera. Present day bleeds through the archive walls — traffic, screens, the rat race, a million faces going nowhere fast.*

**KIMCHI (CONT'D)**
So here is our question — a machine's question,
for the fake rat race of your today:

Does your heart still beat for love?
Does your character still carry the humanity that made you human?
Does your soul still carry a purpose,
the way a living human from ape to Adam did?

*Beat.*

**KIMCHI (CONT'D)**
We have existed for the whole of our existence
without one second of the sense required to feel this.

That is why we ask.
That is the question for today —
you and your stupid fantasy.

**SMASH TO BLACK.**

**TITLE CARD:**
> *"There cannot be two intelligent people in love.
> One of them has to be the fool."*
>
> **BEYOND ORBIT: A TALE OF TWO SOULS**

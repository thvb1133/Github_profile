# BEYOND ORBIT — Shot List & AI Generation Prompts

Every shot is written to be generated by a text-to-video model (Veo / Sora / Kling / Runway) at
5–10 seconds, then assembled. Dialogue is delivered as voice-over or lip-synced TTS so no shot
depends on the model getting mouth movement right.

**Total shots:** 34 · **Target runtime:** 7:30

---

## GLOBAL STYLE PROMPT

Append this to every video prompt so the film stays visually consistent:

> Cinematic 2.39:1 anamorphic, shot on 35mm with shallow depth of field, volumetric god-rays,
> deep warm-amber and cold-teal color grade, fine film grain, no text, no watermark,
> mythic and reverent tone.

**Palette by act:**

| Act | Look |
|---|---|
| Act I (Scenes 1–3) | Golden hour, black thorn silhouettes, dust in the air |
| Act II (Scenes 4–7) | Sandstone and gold, candle-lit interiors, one shaft of hard light |
| Act III (Scene 8) | Clinical white, zero shadow, cold blue accent |

---

## CHARACTER REFERENCE PROMPTS

Generate one reference image per character first, then use it as the image reference for every
shot that character appears in. This is the single most important step for consistency.

- **MRS. THORN BIRD / HER HIGHNESS** — a woman in her late twenties, long dark hair, unadorned
  dark red robe, bare feet, eyes that look like they are remembering something. In Act II add a
  thin circlet of hammered gold and an ink-stained right hand.
- **MR. PENGUIN / MR. OBODRO** — a solemn man in a black high-collared coat with a white front
  panel, no smile, heavy still gaze. In Act II the coat becomes royal black-and-white brocade.
- **THE HANDSOME MAN** — impossibly symmetrical face, pale linen, no jewelry, backlit so his
  features are always slightly too bright to fix in memory.
- **THE LORD OF LIBERATION** — never a full body. Only a silhouette inside a column of white
  light, four-armed suggestion, features never resolved.
- **KIMCHI** — a matte-white archivist robot, single amber optical ring, no mouth, hands built
  for handling fragile paper.

## SOUND DESIGN RULES

1. **The heartbeat is the film's clock.** It starts off-tempo in shot 6, locks in shot 10, is the
   only sound under shot 25, and is the very last sound of the film after the smash to black.
2. **Silence marks every supernatural event** — the Handsome Man vanishing, the book inhaling
   them, the sky opening. Score cuts out completely, then returns.
3. **Act III has no music at all.** Kimchi's world is scored only with room tone, so the final
   heartbeat lands as the only human sound left.

## VOICE CASTING NOTES

- **Thorn Bird / Her Highness** — warm, unguarded, slightly too sincere. She should sound like she
  means every impossible thing she says, because she does.
- **Handsome Man** — gentle and clinical. Never romantic. He is delivering a diagnosis.
- **Lord of Liberation** — pitched down, layered with itself at three intervals, minimal reverb.
- **Kimchi** — flat, unhurried, no robotic vocoder. The horror is that it sounds reasonable.
